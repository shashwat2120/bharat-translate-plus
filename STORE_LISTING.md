## Detailed description
Read any website in your own language, in simple words.

Bharat Translate+ doesn't just translate word-for-word. It first rewrites long, difficult paragraphs in easy language, then translates them into your language. Government notices, legal terms, health information and news become much easier to understand.

✅ Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali
✅ Translate a whole page with one click, then switch back to the original anytime
✅ "Simplify" mode turns hard text into easy words
✅ Right-click any selected text to translate it
✅ Read aloud in your language
✅ 100% private: runs on your computer with Chrome's built-in AI. Your text never leaves your device.
✅ Free. No sign-up. No ads.
✅ Screen-reader friendly and keyboard accessible

Who it's for:
• Anyone more comfortable reading in their mother tongue
• Parents and grandparents using government and banking websites
• Students reading English study material
• People with low literacy or reading difficulties

Requirements: Chrome 138+ on a desktop or laptop. AI models download once on first use. Simplifying needs a reasonably powerful computer; translation works on most.

## Permission justifications
- **host permission (<all_urls>):** Needed to read the text of the page the user chooses to translate and to show the translated text in place. Only runs when the user clicks "Translate this page" or "Show original".
- **scripting:** Injects the code that reads page text and writes back the translation, only on user click.
- **sidePanel:** Shows the extension's controls beside the page.
- **storage:** Saves the user's language choice and "Simplify" setting locally.
- **contextMenus:** Adds "Translate & simplify" to the right-click menu for selected text.
- **Remote code:** None. All code is in the package.
