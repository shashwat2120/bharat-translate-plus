# Privacy Policy — Bharat Translate+

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Bharat Translate+ is built to keep what you read private.

**What the extension reads**
When you click "Translate this page" or "Translate text", the extension reads the text of the current web page or the text you entered. It does this only when you ask it to.

**Where your text goes**
Nowhere. All translation and simplification happens on your own computer using AI models built into Google Chrome. Your text is never sent to us or to any server we run. We do not have a server.

**What we store**
Only your settings (chosen language and whether "Simplify" is on), saved in Chrome on your computer. Text you translate is not saved.

**What we never do**
- No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads
- We don't sell or share any data
- We don't collect personal information

**Permissions and why we need them**
- *Read and change data on websites:* to read the page text and show the translation in place. Used only when you click a button.
- *Side panel:* to show the extension's controls next to the page.
- *Storage:* to remember your language choice.
- *Context menus:* to add "Translate & simplify" to the right-click menu.

**Chrome's built-in AI**
The AI models are provided and updated by Google Chrome. See Google's Chrome privacy notice for how Chrome manages them.
